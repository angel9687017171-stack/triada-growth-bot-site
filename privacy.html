Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Triada Growth Bot is a private analytics tool designed to help analyze TikTok content and improve organic music promotion.

Information We Access

With your permission, the app may access basic TikTok account information and content-related data provided through official TikTok APIs.

How We Use Information

Information is used only for content analytics, performance measurement, and controlled growth experiments.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Data Storage

Application data is primarily processed and stored locally by the app owner.

Authorization

Users can revoke TikTok authorization at any time through their TikTok account settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the app owner through the official Triada Growth Bot project page.
